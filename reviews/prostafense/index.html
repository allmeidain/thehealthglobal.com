What Is ProstAfense And How Does It Work?

You are not sleeping badly. You are sleeping in ninety-minute installments, and there is a difference.

That is the midnight tax. It gets charged nightly, it compounds, and most men pay it for years after quietly deciding it is just part of getting older.

That is the whole mechanism behind the weak stream, the sense of not being finished, and the trip at three in the morning.
