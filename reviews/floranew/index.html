What Is FloraNew And How Does It Work?

If you have taken probiotics before and felt nothing, there is a decent chance you never actually took them.

Here is what that means. Live probiotic strains are fragile, and your stomach is an acid bath running around pH 2 — strong enough to dissolve meat. A standard capsule opens there. The bacteria inside are killed on arrival, and what reaches your intestine, where they were supposed to work, is the equivalent of an empty delivery truck. You paid for live cultures and absorbed dead ones.

That is the delivery problem, and it is the single reason most people conclude probiotics do not work for them.

FloraNew is built around solving it. The capsule uses a delayed-release, acid-resistant coating engineered to survive the stomach intact and open in the lower intestine — where live strains and prebiotic fiber are released directly into the colon. The cargo actually arrives.

What happens then is the part people feel first. A rebalanced gut microbiome stops producing the fermentation gas responsible for that hard, distended bloating — the kind where you look four months pregnant by three in the afternoon and nothing in your closet is comfortable. Digestion smooths out. Transit becomes regular without cramping and without laxatives.

The second effect is slower and matters more. Gut balance is tied to how the body handles stored visceral fat — the deep abdominal fat that sits behind the muscle wall and refuses to respond to ordinary dieting. FloraNew pairs the probiotic delivery with berberine, green tea, and chromium to support that metabolic side.

The manufacturer recommends 90 to 180 days of continuous use. Bloating tends to answer early. The waistline takes longer.
